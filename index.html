अर्धाक्षर (हलंत Key: D)

किसी भी अक्षर के बाद D (्) दबाने पर वह आधा हो जाता है:

क्या = K + D + / + E प्यार = H + D + / + E + J स्थान = M + D + ⇧L + E + V उज्ज्वल = ⇧G + P + D + P + B + N

Shift संयुक्ताक्षर (Single Shift Keys)

ये वर्ण सीधे Shift + Key से टाइप होते हैं:

क्ष = ⇧& त्र = ⇧^ ज्ञ = ⇧% श्र = ⇧* ऋ = ⇧+ ः = ⇧_ ँ = ⇧X । = ⇧> ्र = ⇧# र् = ⇧$

मात्राएं (Matras) & Shift स्वर

मात्रा एवं स्वर कुंजी संदर्भ:

ा = E ि = F ी = R ु = G ू = T ृ = = े = S ै = W ो = A ौ = Q ं = X ॉ = \

Home Row & Finger Placements

Basic home row finger rest positions:

Left Hand: A (Pinky) · S (Ring) · D (Mid) · F (Index) Right Hand: J (Index) · K (Mid) · L (Ring) · ; (Pinky) Thumbs: Spacebar (Left or Right Thumb) Index Reaches: F → R, T, G, V, B | J → U, Y, H, N, M

Opposite Shift Key Rule (Fast Caps)

Use opposite hand shift for effortless capital letters:

Right-Hand Capitals (P, O, I, U, Y, K, L, J, M): Press Left Shift Left-Hand Capitals (Q, W, E, R, T, A, S, D, F, Z, X, C): Press Right Shift Punctuation: ⇧" (Quote) · ⇧: (Colon) · ⇧? (Question)

Exam Speed & Precision Habits

Essential exam accuracy principles:

Spacing Rule: Single Space after comma/period, never before Rhythm: Keep continuous finger flow; don't pause after words 95%+ Target: Speed naturally surges once mistakes are below 5%
